02 End-to-End Field Workflow — the core business loop

Every record flows through this pipeline: unknown market → discovered candidate → qualified lead → opportunity → visited, evidenced, closed.

1

Discover prospects GIS Analyst / Manager Live

Candidates enter from external sources (web research, partner lists) or manual capture. Coordinates are validated against Hyderabad bounds and auto-corrected if missing.

Discovery modulePOST /discovery-candidatesGIS validation status
2

Duplicate screening System Live

Fuzzy name/address matching against existing Leads and Accounts flags duplicates before anything pollutes the CRM.

duplicate_detection.pyPOST .../check-duplicates
3

Convert candidate → Lead Manager / Analyst Live

One click creates the Lead in Salesforce carrying validated coordinates, review notes and source metadata.

POST .../convert-to-leadLeads module
4

Auto territory assignment System Live

Managers draw territory boundaries directly on the GIS map. Every new Account / Lead / Candidate is assigned by point-in-polygon test — no manual tagging.

Territories moduleleaflet-draw polygonsterritory_assignment_service
5

Qualify → Opportunity Sales Exec / Rep Live

Contact and qualification happens on the ground; converted Leads become Opportunities tracked through stages toward close.

Leads → Opportunities modulesFull edit capability
6

Plan & optimize the day's route Field Rep Live

Pick today's stops inside your territory; OpenRouteService computes real driving order, distance and time — replacing the old simulated routing.

Routes moduleGET /routes/todayManager approve / reject
7

GPS geofence check-in / check-out Field Rep Live

Arrive at the stop, browser GPS fixes position; check-in is only accepted within the customer geofence. Check-out records duration. Visit reopens automatically if GIS validation is not “Validated”.

FieldVisits modulePOST /visitsWebSocket broadcast → live dashboard
8

Capture evidence Field Rep Live

Photos, notes and documents attach to the visit and its Account / Lead with a validation status trail — proof of every meeting.

Evidence module/evidence/visit/{id}
9

Measure & act Leadership Live

The Dashboard aggregates all nine modules — pipeline by status/source/month, visit outcomes, overdue accounts, route distance/time, territory activity — plus an Executive Analytics panel. Notifications and the activity feed are driven by real backend events, not mock data.

/dashboard/* (20+ aggregates)ExecutiveAnalyticsPanelRecharts + PDF export

05 Realtime event flow

Write operations push instant updates to every open session — no refresh needed.

1 · Action happens

  • Rep checks in at a stop
  • Account created / updated

2 · Router broadcasts

  • salesforce_router → realtime.py

3 · All clients update
